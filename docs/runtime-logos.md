# Runtime icon provenance and launch follow-ups

The 35 runtime labels use recognizable project or company icons. They identify guidance and official destinations without asserting SDK compatibility, certification, endorsement, or a commercial relationship.

## Public-launch responsibility

Before public launch, the developer must resolve the agreed permission review for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and IBM, and check the restrictions published by AWS, Microsoft, and GitHub. Shipping an asset here does not establish permission. Permission requests and decisions remain the developer's responsibility. Preserve attribution, clear space, proportions, colors, and any conditions attached to permission. Do not mechanically recolor or invert a mark to create a dark variant.

The table links published usage guidance where located. References marked “project reference” identify the publisher, **not a logo usage grant**; separate brand terms were not located during this inspection. Review those terms before launch as well. Sources were inspected on 2026-10-12.

## Asset sources

Assets live in `projects/website-ui/runtime-logos/` and are exported through `@moldea.ai/website-ui/runtime-logos/*`. SVG extraction removes executable or embedding constructs, presentation classes, metadata, and unused attributes while retaining geometry. Source-provided theme variants are preferred. PNG bytes are preserved. Unit checks reject unsafe SVG constructs and oversized raster assets.

## Runtime mapping and presentation

`projects/website-ui/src/runtime-catalog/constants.ts` owns the exact ID, display name, official destination, asset, and theme mapping. `custom` is absent from the company inventory and remains a manifest option. Strands uses its dark-ink asset in the light theme and gray-ink asset in the dark theme.

The shared Astro grid and platform React composition show three responsive rows initially. Load more reveals three additional rows. Every runtime opens its official website or repository in a new tab with an external-link icon. All 35 links remain available without JavaScript. No SDK support or vendor permission is inferred from inclusion.
